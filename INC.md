| [Qilin and INC Ransomware Groups Exploit Palo Alto Networks Flaw to Bypass MFA – The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/qilin-and-inc-ransomware-groups-exploit.html) | 20 Oct 2026 | The Hacker News reported that Qilin (tracked as Spikey Scorpius) and INC Ransomware (tracked as Tarnished Scorpius) affiliates were exploiting CVE-2026-0257, an authentication-bypass vulnerability affecting Palo Alto Networks PAN-OS and Prisma Access. The flaw can enable unauthorized VPN connections and bypass MFA protections. Following initial access, attackers were observed conducting network reconnaissance, moving laterally through RDP and SMB, harvesting credentials and exfiltrating data before deploying ransomware. Researchers also documented attacks targeting virtualization infrastructure, including VMware vCenter and ESXi, to evade endpoint security and obtain domain credentials. The vulnerability was patched in May 2026, and CISA added it to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) catalog on 13 May 2026. |
